    How to play

    1. 1Drag across the letters to find the hidden words.
    2. 2You can drag in any direction, but only along the given paths.
    3. 3Letters and paths disappear when they are no longer needed.
    4. 4Words can be found in any order and the puzzle stays solvable.

    Controls

    1. 1Drag across letters and release to submit a word.
    2. 2Or tap letters one by one, then tap the word above to submit.

    Settings

    Word settings apply to newly generated puzzles.
